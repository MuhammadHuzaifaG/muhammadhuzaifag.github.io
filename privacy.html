Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit this website or contact me.

Effective date: November 18, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Muhammad Huzaifa (“I,” “me,” or “my”) handles information in connection with this portfolio website. By using the website, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the website.

If you contact me through a form or email, you may provide information such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message. Please do not send sensitive personal information through the website.

When you visit the website, your browser or hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser and device type, requested pages, and access times, to deliver and protect the website. The website may also store your display-theme preference in your browser’s local storage.

Information you submit is used to respond to your enquiries and provide requested services. Information may also be used to operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the website, and to meet applicable legal obligations.

The website relies on third-party providers for certain features, such as hosting, email delivery, fonts, icons, and interactive services. This website may use browser local storage to remember your selected light or dark theme. Third-party services or the website’s hosting infrastructure may use cookies or similar technologies for their own operation. You can manage cookies and local storage through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some features.

7. Disclosure and retention

Personal information is not sold. It may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate the website or respond to you, or disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights and security. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes, unless a longer period is required by law.

8. Security and your choices

Reasonable measures are used to protect information, but no method of online transmission or storage is completely secure. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact me to make a request.

9. Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact me so it can be addressed.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, please use the contact form.
